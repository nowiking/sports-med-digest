UCB-MSCs-HA植入5年疗效优于微骨折：VAS多降22.9分

More Durable Cartilage Repair and Better Clinical Outcomes at 5 Years Using Allogeneic Umbilical Cord Blood-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells and Hyaluronate Composite Implantation Versus Microfracture for Knee Osteoarthritis.

Arthroscopy · 2026-10-31 · Ⅲ期RCT回顾性子集分析 · PMID 42965407

一句话结论：在膝骨关节炎患者中，与微骨折相比，同种异体脐带血间充质干细胞-透明质酸复合物植入在5年时疼痛VAS多降低22.9分、WOMAC多改善15.9分，并获更持久的MRI修复组织。

要点：5年随访时，UCB-MSCs-HA组VAS为23.4 vs 微骨折组46.3（P=0.0019），WOMAC为21.8 vs 37.7（P=0.016），IKDC亦显著更优；MRI MOCART评分在3年和5年均更高，持久修复比例更高。

对临床的启示：该研究为UCB-MSCs-HA复合物作为膝OA生物修复选项提供了5年证据，提示其可能延缓或替代部分微骨折手术。但作为Ⅲ期试验的回顾性子集分析，样本量有限（48例完成5年随访），且缺乏组织学验证，推广前需权衡成本与适应证。

Conclusion: At 5 years, allogeneic UCB-MSCs-HA composite implantation provided greater pain relief (VAS 23.4 vs 46.3), better function (WOMAC 21.8 vs 37.7), and more durable MRI-based cartilage repair than microfracture in knee osteoarthritis.

Key points: At 5 years, UCB-MSCs-HA versus microfracture: VAS 23.4 vs 46.3 (P=0.0019); WOMAC 21.8 vs 37.7 (P=0.016); IKDC significantly better; higher MOCART scores at 3 and 5 years with more durable repair.

Clinical implications: UCB-MSCs-HA composite implantation offers a promising biologic option for knee osteoarthritis with 5-year superiority over microfracture. However, this retrospective subset analysis of a phase 3 trial has a limited sample (48 completers) and lacks histological confirmation; cost and patient selection must be considered before broader adoption.
