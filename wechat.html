Arthroscopy · 2026-10-31 · 回顾性子集分析 · PMID 42965407

一句话结论：在Kellgren-Lawrence I-III级膝骨关节炎中，与微骨折相比，异体脐带血MSC-透明质酸复合物植入在5年时VAS（23.4 vs 46.3）、WOMAC（21.8 vs 37.7）与IKDC（59.4 vs 46.5）均显著更优，且MRI修复组织更持久。

要点：48例完成5年随访。复合物组VAS、WOMAC、IKDC均显著优于微骨折组（P分别为0.0019、0.016、0.014）；VAS达MCID比例50.0% vs 28.1%（P=0.123，未达显著）。以MOCART>60且间隔变化<10分定义为持久修复，复合物组比例更高。

对临床的启示：该研究为生物修复在中长期结构与症状维持上提供了对照证据，支持对轻中度膝骨关节炎、希望延迟关节置换的活跃患者考虑MSC复合物植入。但本研究为III期试验的回顾性子集分析，完成5年随访仅48例，存在选择偏倚与样本量限制，MCID差异未达统计学显著，且缺乏组织学验证，结论仍需更大样本前瞻性研究确认。

Conclusion: Allogeneic umbilical cord blood-derived MSC-hyaluronate composite implantation yielded more durable MRI repair tissue and superior 5-year pain and functional scores than microfracture in Kellgren-Lawrence grade I-III knee osteoarthritis.

Key points: Of 48 patients completing 5-year follow-up, the composite group showed significantly better VAS (23.4 vs 46.3), WOMAC (21.8 vs 37.7) and IKDC (59.4 vs 46.5) scores than microfracture; durable repair (MOCART >60 with <10-point interval change) was more frequent, while the MCID achievement difference for VAS (50.0% vs 28.1%) was not statistically significant.

Clinical implications: These findings support MSC-hyaluronate composite implantation as a biologic option for mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis in active patients seeking to delay arthroplasty. However, this retrospective subset analysis of a phase 3 trial included only 48 patients at 5 years, carrying selection bias and limited power; the MCID difference was not significant and no histological confirmation was available, so larger prospective studies are needed.

Two developments have challenged and extended contemporary fueling practice: a shift toward carbohydrate intakes of 100 to 150 g/h in elite endurance sport, and widespread commercial availability of exogenous ketone supplements marketed as ergogenic aids. Both have outpaced the clinical evidence and have reached the competitive and recreational athletes who present to sports medicine clinicians. Narrative review of primary metabolic and performance studies, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and consensus statements published through mid-2026, identified through PubMed and reference tracing. Narrative review. Level 5. Multiple-transportable-carbohydrate formulations (glucose plus fructose) raise exogenous carbohydrate oxidation above the roughly 1 g/minute ceiling imposed by intestinal glucose transport, providing the mechanistic basis for fueling rates of ≥90 g/h.
