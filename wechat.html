关节镜下关节囊松解在冻结肩术后6个月内活动度改善优于非手术治疗

Arthroscopic Capsular Release Provides Greater Range-of-Motion Improvement Within the First 6 Months Postoperatively Than Nonoperative Management Modalities in Frozen Shoulder: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.

Arthroscopy · 2026-10-28 · 系统综述+Meta分析 · PMID 42952285

一句话结论：纳入6项RCT共705例冻结肩患者，关节镜下关节囊松解在术后6个月内活动度改善达到最小临床重要差异，但改善幅度随时间衰减；疼痛仅在6个月时出现统计学显著但未达临床意义的改善，安全性与非手术治疗相当。

要点：证据确定性为极低至中等；活动度改善在6个月内达MCID但幅度递减；疼痛改善在6个月时统计学显著但未达临床意义；其他随访时间点无显著疼痛改善；两组安全性相当。

对临床的启示：对于冻结肩患者，关节镜下关节囊松解可作为早期（6个月内）改善活动度的有效选择，但需告知患者获益随时间衰减，且疼痛缓解有限。临床决策应结合病程阶段、患者对保守治疗的应答及手术风险偏好。本综述证据确定性偏低，主要受限于RCT样本量、干预异质性及随访时长；未来需设计更严谨的RCT明确最佳手术时机与术后康复方案。

Conclusion: Arthroscopic capsular release may provide superior range-of-motion improvement within the first 6 months postoperatively compared with nonoperative management in frozen shoulder, but the magnitude diminishes over time and pain benefits are not clinically meaningful.

Key points: Six RCTs (705 patients) with very low to moderate certainty evidence; range-of-motion improvement reached MCID within 6 months but diminished over time; pain improvement at 6 months was statistically significant but not clinically meaningful; safety profiles were comparable.

Clinical implications: Arthroscopic capsular release can be considered for early range-of-motion improvement in frozen shoulder, but patients should be counseled about diminishing benefits and limited pain relief. Evidence certainty is low; optimal timing and rehabilitation remain to be defined.
