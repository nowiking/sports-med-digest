回顾性队列 · IV级证据 · 265例 · 随访≥6年

改良 MacIntosh 髂胫束 + 股薄肌增强 ACL 重建：6 年再断裂率仅 4.9%

Low Graft Re-Rupture Rate After Primary ACL Reconstruction Using a Modified MacIntosh Iliotibial Band Technique With Gracilis Augmentation: Minimum 6-Year Follow-up

Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc · 2026-09-23 · 法国 FAST 队列 · PMID 42775564

一句话结论：265 例初次 ACL 重建（改良 MacIntosh 髂胫束技术 + 股薄肌增强，2015-2019），平均随访 84.3±10.7 个月。移植物再断裂 13 例（4.9%）；非失败并发症 5 例（1.9%）；额外手术（不含翻修）13 例（4.9%），以继发半月板病变切除最常见（4.2%）。252 例功能分析显著改善（P<.001），中位 IKDC 92.0、Lysholm 95.0。249 例术前运动者中 228 例（91.6%）重返运动，154 例（61.8%）重返同一运动、69 例（44.8%）恢复伤前水平，中位重返时间 8.0 个月。更年轻与内侧半月板撕裂为再断裂的独立危险因素。

要点：来自 FAST 队列（法国前瞻性 ACL 重建队列研究）的单中心回顾性分析，纳入 2015-01 至 2019-10 行该术式的初次重建者。主要结局为移植物再断裂；末次随访评估 PROMs、重返运动与并发症；多因素 logistic 回归识别再断裂及恢复伤前水平的相关因素。

对临床的启示：「关节内 + 关节外」联合重建近年重新升温，这项 6 年数据给出了支持：4.9% 再断裂、IKDC 92/Lysholm 95、91.6% 重返运动，整体表现扎实。最值得注意的是一种不牺牲腘绳肌腱（保留其屈膝/内旋动力）、也不使用异体的 ACL 重建选项——与上一期「单纯 BPTB 失败率更高」的结论相呼应，这种髂胫束联合重建可作为年轻高需求患者的讨论方案之一。局限是 IV 级证据、单中心、无对照组。

原文：doi.org/10.1002/ksa.70626

肱二头肌长头腱关节内动态前向稳定：全缝线锚钉免植骨新术式

Inlay Dynamic Anterior Stabilization of the Shoulder With Long Head Biceps Tendon Using All-Suture Anchor

Arthroscopy Techniques · 2026-09-22 · 技术说明 · PMID 42775317

一句话结论：肱二头肌长头腱（LHBT）动态前向稳定已作为前向肩关节不稳（亚临界关节盂骨缺损）的软组织替代方案兴起。本文描述一种关节内（inlay）动态前向稳定技术：改良 Bankart 修复 + 经肩胛下肌 LHBT 转位，以全缝线锚钉固定于 5 mm 前下关节盂骨道、Krakow 缝合，重建「吊索 + 吊床」稳定效应，同时保留外旋、避免植骨。盂唇组织不足以单独 Bankart 修复时可术中灵活切换，且不阻碍未来骨块手术。

▍Knee · 2 articles

Retrospective Cohort · Level IV · 265 pts · ≥6-yr FU

Low Graft Re-Rupture Rate After Primary ACL Reconstruction Using a Modified MacIntosh Iliotibial Band Technique With Gracilis Augmentation: Minimum 6-Year Follow-up

改良 MacIntosh 髂胫束 + 股薄肌增强 ACL 重建：6 年再断裂率仅 4.9%

Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc · 2026-09-23 · 法国 FAST 队列 · PMID 42775564

Conclusion: In 265 primary ACL reconstructions (modified MacIntosh ITB technique with gracilis augmentation, 2015–2019; mean follow-up 84.3±10.7 months), graft re-rupture occurred in 13 (4.9%), non-failure complications in 5 (1.9%), and additional non-revision surgery in 13 (4.9%), most commonly meniscectomy for secondary meniscal lesions (4.2%). Functional outcomes (252 patients) improved significantly (P<.001) with median IKDC 92.0 and Lysholm 95.0. Of 249 pre-injury athletes, 228 (91.6%) returned to sport, 154 (61.8%) to the same sport, and 69 (44.8%) to their pre-injury level; median RTS was 8.0 months. Younger age and medial meniscal tear were independent predictors of re-rupture.

Key points: Single-center retrospective analysis from the FAST Cohort (French Prospective ACL Reconstruction Cohort Study), 2015–2019. Primary outcome: graft re-rupture; PROMs, RTS and complications at final follow-up; multivariable logistic regression.

Clinical implications: Combined intra- and extra-articular reconstruction has regained interest, and this 6-year series supports it: 4.9% re-rupture, IKDC 92/Lysholm 95, and 91.6% RTS. Most notable is an ACL option that spares the hamstrings (preserving flexion/internal-rotation power) without allograft—echoing last issue's finding of higher isolated-BPTB failure, this ITB-based combined reconstruction merits discussion for young, high-demand patients. Limitations: Level IV, single-center, no control group.

Source: doi.org/10.1002/ksa.70626

Technical Note · Arthroscopic Technique

The Direct Medial and Lateral Inferior Horizontal Portals of the Knee: A Modified Arthroscopic Approach for Anterior Horn Horizontal Tears

膝关节前角水平撕裂：直接内外侧下入路让深部清创不再费力

Arthroscopy Techniques · 2026-09-22 · 技术说明 · PMID 42775280

Conclusion: Mild horizontal tears in the meniscal avascular zone are common findings in complex or discoid meniscus surgery, but debriding the inferior surface in the anterior one-half to one-third is difficult owing to visualization and instrument limitations. This note describes direct medial and lateral inferior portals inferior to the anterior one-third of the meniscal body, enabling efficient deep horizontal-tear debridement with shavers or rongeurs, improving observation, efficiency and reducing difficulty.

Key points: Technical note on portal placement (inferior to the anterior one-third of the meniscal body) and debridement technique for deep horizontal tears in complex/discoid meniscus surgery.

Clinical implications: Debriding anterior-horn horizontal tears is a step many surgeons skip because it is awkward, yet residual unstable tear edges may cause ongoing mechanical symptoms. The direct inferior portals described here are a pragmatic trick using standard instruments—no special equipment—worth mastering for routine complex meniscus surgery. As a technical note, no outcome data.

Source: doi.org/10.1002/atn2.70283

Technical Note · Arthroscopic Technique

Inlay Dynamic Anterior Stabilization of the Shoulder With Long Head Biceps Tendon Using All-Suture Anchor

肱二头肌长头腱关节内动态前向稳定：全缝线锚钉免植骨新术式

Arthroscopy Techniques · 2026-09-22 · 技术说明 · PMID 42775317

Conclusion: Dynamic anterior stabilization using the long head of the biceps tendon (LHBT) has emerged as a soft-tissue alternative for anterior instability with subcritical glenoid bone loss. This note describes an inlay technique combining a modified Bankart repair with trans-subscapularis LHBT transfer fixed within a 5-mm anteroinferior glenoid bone tunnel using all-suture anchors and Krakow suturing, reproducing 'sling + hammock' stabilization while preserving external rotation and avoiding bone grafting. It allows intraoperative adaptability when labral tissue is inadequate and does not preclude future bone-block procedures.

Key points: Technical note detailing portals, anchor placement, tendon transfer/fixation, and indications (glenoid bone loss <20%).

Clinical implications: LHBT dynamic anterior stabilization—a graft-free soft-tissue option—offers a low-cost, revisable choice for young patients with critical bone loss who decline grafting. The 'inlay + all-suture anchor' detail here optimizes tendon-to-bone healing and length-tension, a refinement of technique. Indications should be strictly limited to glenoid bone loss <20% with acceptable labral quality. As a technical note it lacks clinical outcomes and warrants caution.

Source: doi.org/10.1002/atn2.70262
