The presence of cement pockets on the tibial component undersurface is associated with reduced revision for aseptic tibial loosening-A single-centre study of 11,594 total knee arthroplasties

Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc · 2026-11-03 · 回顾性队列 · PMID 42975568

一句话结论：在11,594例初次骨水泥型全膝关节置换中，胫骨托底面骨水泥袋设计对全因翻修无显著影响（p=0.67），但显著降低无菌性胫骨松动翻修率，固定平台与活动平台亚组结果一致。

要点：①纳入2003-2023年连续11,594例，最短随访2年；②固定平台8591例（含袋4753例）、活动平台3003例（含袋942例）；③全因翻修无差异（p=0.67）；④无菌性胫骨松动翻修在含袋组显著降低。

对临床的启示：该研究为假体界面设计提供了大样本真实世界证据，提示对骨质疏松或胫骨侧应力较大者可优先考虑带骨水泥袋的胫骨托。但属单中心回顾性设计，存在术者与年代混杂，且缺乏影像学松动与骨密度分层数据，结论宜作为假体选择参考而非绝对标准。

Conclusion: Cement pockets on the tibial component undersurface did not affect all-cause revision but were associated with significantly reduced revision for aseptic tibial loosening in 11,594 primary cemented TKAs.

Key points: 1) 11,594 consecutive cemented TKAs, minimum 2-year follow-up; 2) 8591 fixed-bearing (4753 with pockets) and 3003 mobile-bearing (942 with pockets); 3) no difference in all-cause revision (p=0.67); 4) significantly lower revision for aseptic tibial loosening with cement pockets.

Clinical implications: These real-world data support considering cement-pocket tibial baseplates in patients at higher risk of tibial loosening, such as those with poor bone stock or high tibial stress. The single-centre retrospective design, era and surgeon confounding, and lack of radiographic loosening and bone-density stratification limit the strength of the inference.
