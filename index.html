女性职业足球ACL损伤率1.69倍于男性，非接触与人工草皮风险更高

Anterior cruciate ligament injuries in women's professional football: Higher incidence and distinct injury patterns compared to men

Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc · 2026-11-17 · 前瞻性队列研究 · PMID 43043086

一句话结论：德国职业足球注册库2016/17至2024/25赛季共459例ACL损伤，女性每1000比赛小时1.19例、男性0.69例（IRR 1.69，95%CI 1.34-2.13），顶级联赛差异最大（IRR 2.49）；女性非接触（aOR 2.37）、间接接触（aOR 2.92）及人工草皮（aOR 5.42）损伤风险显著更高。

要点：①女性ACL损伤率显著高于男性，且差异集中于顶级联赛；②女性以非接触与间接接触机制为主，提示动作控制与神经肌肉训练缺口；③人工草皮与国家队球员身份与女性损伤风险独立相关；④合并损伤谱两性总体相似，仅女性外侧副韧带损伤较少。

对临床的启示：该研究将性别差异从“损伤率”推进到“机制与场景”，支持在女足顶级联赛优先落地针对性神经肌肉预防方案，并对人工草皮比赛负荷与鞋钉选择提出警示。结合本期Meta分析（性别不影响ACLR后移植物失败与功能恢复），差异主要发生在损伤发生端，预防窗口应前移至赛季前与训练日常。局限：注册库依赖俱乐部上报，暴露时间与草皮类型为粗分类，缺乏生物力学与激素周期数据，无法推断因果。

Conclusion: ACL injury rates are higher in women's professional football, particularly in the first division, with a predominance of non-contact and indirect-contact mechanisms and artificial turf exposure, supporting sex-specific prevention.

Key points: ①Female players had a significantly higher ACL injury rate, especially in the first division. ②Non-contact and indirect-contact mechanisms predominated in women. ③Artificial turf and national-team status were independently associated with injury in women. ④Concomitant injury patterns were broadly similar between sexes, except fewer lateral collateral ligament injuries in women.

Clinical implications: These findings move the sex difference beyond incidence toward mechanisms and contexts, supporting targeted neuromuscular prevention in elite women's football and raising caution regarding artificial turf and footwear. Coupled with the present meta-analysis showing no sex effect on graft failure or functional recovery after ACLR, the disparity originates at the injury-occurrence end, so prevention should start in preseason and daily training. Limitations include registry-based reporting, coarse exposure and surface classification, and no biomechanical or hormonal data, precluding causal inference.
