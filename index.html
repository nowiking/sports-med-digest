股四头肌腱与腘绳肌腱重建ACL：临床结局相当但力量恢复轨迹不同

Quadriceps and hamstring tendon autografts show similar clinical outcomes but distinct strength-recovery profiles after primary anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction: A meta-analysis.

J ISAKOS · 2026-11-14 · 系统综述与Meta分析 · PMID 43033720

一句话结论：纳入15项RCT共936例，股四头肌腱与腘绳肌腱自体移植物在24个月IKDC评分无显著差异（MD=-1.1），但供区力量恢复轨迹不同，需个体化选择。

要点：①临床结局：24个月IKDC评分组间无统计学差异（MD=-1.1），膝关节稳定性与功能表现相当；②力量恢复：股四头肌腱组伸膝力量恢复更优，腘绳肌腱组屈膝力量恢复更优，供区病率谱不同；③证据质量：GRADE评估部分结局确定性为中至低，随访多限于12-24个月。

对临床的启示：该Meta分析为ACL重建移植物选择提供了量化依据：两种移植物临床结局相当，但力量恢复轨迹不同。临床决策应结合患者运动需求、力量恢复目标及供区病率偏好个体化选择。需注意多数研究随访仅12-24个月，缺乏长期再断裂率与骨关节炎进展数据，且未按运动水平分层，对精英运动员的指导价值有限。

Conclusion: Quadriceps and hamstring tendon autografts yield comparable 24-month IKDC scores (MD=-1.1) after primary ACLR, but exhibit distinct strength-recovery profiles, supporting individualized graft selection.

Key points: ①Clinical outcomes: no significant difference in 24-month IKDC score (MD=-1.1); knee stability and functional performance comparable. ②Strength recovery: quadriceps graft favored extensor strength recovery, hamstring graft favored flexor strength recovery, with distinct donor-site morbidity profiles. ③Evidence quality: GRADE certainty moderate to low for some outcomes; follow-up mostly 12-24 months.

Clinical implications: This meta-analysis provides quantitative guidance for graft choice in ACLR: clinical outcomes are comparable, but strength-recovery trajectories differ. Decision-making should be individualized based on sport demands, strength-recovery goals, and donor-site morbidity preferences. Limitations include short follow-up (12-24 months), lack of long-term reinjury and osteoarthritis data, and no stratification by sport level, limiting guidance for elite athletes.
