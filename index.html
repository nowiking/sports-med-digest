关节镜解剖型肩盂重建联合Remplissage治疗肩关节不稳伴骨丢失

Arthroscopic Anatomic Glenoid Reconstruction With Distal Tibial Allograft and Remplissage Suture Staple Procedure in the Beach-Chair Position

Arthrosc Tech · 2026-11-08 · 技术说明 · PMID 43005470

一句话结论：针对复发性肩关节不稳伴临界骨丢失及Hill-Sachs损伤，采用关节镜解剖型肩盂重建（远端胫骨异体骨块）联合无结Remplissage缝合锚钉技术，在沙滩椅体位下可同时处理骨与软组织病变，生物力学研究提示联合Remplissage可提高重建后稳定性。

要点：①关节镜解剖型肩盂重建采用远端胫骨异体骨块恢复肩盂骨性结构；②Remplissage通过后关节囊及冈下肌腱填充Hill-Sachs缺损；③无结缝合锚钉技术简化操作，降低结节相关并发症；④沙滩椅体位便于同时处理前方骨丢失与后方Hill-Sachs损伤。

对临床的启示：对于复发性肩关节不稳合并临界骨丢失及on-track/off-track Hill-Sachs损伤的患者，单纯骨块重建可能不足以恢复稳定性，联合Remplissage可提供额外的软组织稳定。该技术说明为临床提供了可操作的全关节镜下方案，但缺乏长期临床随访数据，其疗效仍需前瞻性队列验证。

Conclusion: Arthroscopic anatomic glenoid reconstruction with distal tibial allograft combined with knotless remplissage is a feasible all-arthroscopic technique for recurrent shoulder instability with critical bone loss and Hill-Sachs lesions, potentially improving stability based on biomechanical evidence.

Key points: ①Arthroscopic anatomic glenoid reconstruction restores glenoid bone stock using distal tibial allograft; ②Remplissage fills Hill-Sachs defects via posterior capsulotenodesis; ③Knotless suture staple simplifies the procedure and reduces knot-related complications; ④Beach-chair position allows concurrent management of anterior bone loss and posterior Hill-Sachs lesions.

Clinical implications: For patients with recurrent shoulder instability, critical bone loss, and engaging Hill-Sachs lesions, glenoid reconstruction alone may be insufficient; adding remplissage may enhance stability. This technique note provides a reproducible all-arthroscopic approach, but long-term clinical outcomes are needed to confirm efficacy.
