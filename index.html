5年随访：UCB-MSCs-HA复合植入较微骨折显著改善膝骨关节炎临床与MRI结局

More Durable Cartilage Repair and Better Clinical Outcomes at 5 Years Using Allogeneic Umbilical Cord Blood-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells and Hyaluronate Composite Implantation Versus Microfracture for Knee Osteoarthritis.

Arthroscopy · 2026-10-31 · 回顾性子集分析 · PMID 42965407

一句话结论：在膝骨关节炎患者中，UCB-MSCs-HA复合植入组5年VAS（23.4 vs 46.3）、WOMAC（21.8 vs 37.7）和IKDC评分均显著优于微骨折组，且MRI显示更持久的软骨修复。

要点：共纳入64例K-L I-III级膝骨关节炎患者，48例完成5年随访。UCB-MSCs-HA组在VAS、WOMAC和IKDC评分上均显著优于微骨折组（P<0.05）。MRI MOCART评分显示UCB-MSCs-HA组修复组织更持久。

对临床的启示：该研究为UCB-MSCs-HA复合植入修复膝骨关节炎软骨提供了5年长期证据，支持其作为微骨折的替代方案。但作为3期试验的回顾性子集分析，样本量较小且非随机对照，结论需谨慎解读。临床决策时应结合患者个体情况，并关注未来更高质量的证据。

Conclusion: At 5 years, UCB-MSCs-HA composite implantation demonstrated significantly better clinical outcomes and more durable cartilage repair compared with microfracture in patients with knee osteoarthritis.

Key points: In this retrospective subset analysis of 64 patients with K-L grade I-III knee osteoarthritis, UCB-MSCs-HA implantation resulted in significantly better VAS, WOMAC, and IKDC scores at 5 years compared with microfracture. MRI MOCART scores indicated more durable repair tissue in the UCB-MSCs-HA group.

Clinical implications: UCB-MSCs-HA composite implantation offers a promising long-term alternative to microfracture for cartilage repair in knee osteoarthritis. However, the retrospective subset design and small sample size warrant cautious interpretation. Clinicians should consider individual patient factors and await further high-quality evidence.

原文 / Source：doi.org/10.1002/arj.70706

髋关节镜患者基线心理韧性仅与心理健康相关，与疼痛和功能无关

Baseline Resilience Correlates With Baseline Mental Health but Not Baseline Pain or Functional Status in Patients Undergoing Surgery for Femoroacetabular Impingement Syndrome.

Arthroscopy · PMID 42965399

摘要：本研究纳入300例因股骨髋臼撞击综合征行髋关节镜的患者（中位年龄32岁，71.7%女性），评估基线心理韧性（CD-RISC-10）与基线疼痛（HOOS-Pain）和功能（HOOS-PS）的关系。多变量线性回归显示，基线心理韧性与HOOS-Pain或HOOS-PS均无显著关联，但与心理健康（VR-12 MCS）呈中度相关（r=0.55）。结论提示，心理韧性主要反映心理健康状态，而非疼痛或功能水平。

This study included 300 patients undergoing hip arthroscopy for femoroacetabular impingement syndrome (median age 32 years, 71.7% women) to evaluate whether baseline resilience correlates with baseline pain and function. Multivariable linear regression showed no significant association between baseline resilience (CD-RISC-10) and HOOS-Pain or HOOS-PS, but a moderate correlation with mental health (VR-12 MCS, r=0.55). Resilience primarily reflects mental health rather than pain or functional status.

原文：doi.org/10.1002/arj.70678

Patellar tendon rupture and chronic extensor mechanism deficiency present significant reconstructive challenges. Subtalar stiffness is a late complication of calcaneal fractures. Open release limits early mobilization due to extensive surgical wounds. This technical note describes endoscopic posterolateral subtalar release, which involves resection of the posterior calcaneal spur and soft-tissue release of the posterior and lateral aspects of the posterior subtalar joint through small incisions, allowing immediate vigorous mobilization. This technique offers a minimally invasive option for subtalar stiffness.

Management of massive rotator cuff tears remains challenging, with direct repair often failing due to excessive tension. This article describes an arthroscopic "Triple-Beam" biologic reconstruction technique combining long head of biceps transposition with autologous palmaris longus tendon augmentation. This cost-effective autologous strategy aims to improve healing and biomechanical stability.

Arthrosc Tech · PMID 42964870

摘要：本文介绍半膜肌肌腱获取及其作为自体移植物重建膝后内侧复合体（PMC）的技术。保留半膜肌远端止点，利用内侧三分之二肌腱以“Romal Reins”概念重建PMC，恢复关节稳定性。该技术安全、简单、微创，尤其适用于多韧带松弛且缺乏游离自体移植物的病例，减少了对多个移植物和骨隧道的需求。

This technical report presents semimembranosus tendon harvest and its use as an autograft for posteromedial complex (PMC) reconstruction using a "Romal Reins" concept. The distal semimembranosus tendon is robust and long, with multiple insertions onto the posteromedial corner. Harvesting the medial two-thirds while leaving the lateral third intact preserves dynamic action. This technique tightens and reconstructs the major PMC stabilizing structures without requiring individual reconstructions, reducing the need for multiple grafts, tunnels, and implants. It is safe, simple, minimally invasive, and effective, particularly in multiligament laxity cases.

The "Minimizing Excessive Rotational Instability Technique" (MERI Technique): A Simple Lateral Tenodesis Reconstructing the Condylar Strap, the Distal Kaplan Fibers, and the Femoral Insertion of the Anterolateral Ligament to Control Internal Rotation During Anterior Cruciate Ligament Surgery.

This article describes the Minimizing Excessive Rotational Instability (MERI) technique, a simple and minimally invasive lateral tenodesis designed to enhance internal rotation control during ACL reconstruction by reconstructing the distal Kaplan fibers, the condylar strap, and the femoral insertion of the anterolateral ligament. Using a 1-cm-wide fascia lata strip, the procedure involves fixation with a single anchor at an isometric point proximal to the anterolateral ligament insertion, followed by a continuous locking suture. This technique balances stability and flexibility, potentially reducing postoperative stiffness and morbidity, and is a cost-effective alternative for anterolateral instability.

Editorial Commentary: Patellar Tendon-Lateral Trochlear Ridge Distance May Outperform Tibial Tubercle-Trochlear Groove for Assessing Patellar Containment, but Trochlear Dysplasia and Patellar Height Are Likely the Culprits.

Arthroscopy · PMID 42963902

摘要：复发性髌股不稳定很少由单一解剖异常解释，滑车发育不良、髌骨高度、扭转对线等均独立增加脱位风险。本评论指出，髌腱-滑车外侧嵴距离可能优于胫骨结节-滑车沟距离来评估髌骨包容性，但比较组间滑车发育不良和髌骨高度的差异可能是混杂因素。强调解剖风险因素常相互关联，测量指标的价值需在解剖特征均衡的组间比较中验证。

Recurrent patellofemoral instability is rarely explained by a single anatomic abnormality; trochlear dysplasia, patellar height, torsional malalignment, and lateral extensor vector each independently increase dislocation risk. This editorial commentary suggests that patellar tendon-lateral trochlear ridge distance may outperform tibial tubercle-trochlear groove distance for assessing patellar containment, but differences in trochlear dysplasia and patellar height between comparison groups may be confounding factors. It emphasizes that anatomic risk factors are interrelated, and the value of measurements should be validated in groups with balanced anatomic features.
