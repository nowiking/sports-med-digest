冻结肩手法松解：二次镜检无关节囊撕裂仍改善活动度

Relative Real-time Arthroscopic Evaluation of Glenohumeral Structural Failure during Manipulation under Anesthesia for Primary Frozen Shoulder.

J Shoulder Elbow Surg · 2026-11-09 · 前瞻性病例系列 · PMID 43003382

一句话结论：31例原发性冻结肩患者行手法松解，二次镜检显示100%出现关节内粘连及上盂肱韧带断裂，但无任何关节囊撕裂，术后6个月活动度及功能评分显著改善。

要点：①二次镜检均见关节内粘连及上盂肱韧带断裂（100%），无关节囊撕裂；②SLAP损伤及前下盂唇撕脱各占12.8%，均无需修复；③术后6个月活动度、VAS及ASES评分显著改善。

对临床的启示：本研究提示手法松解恢复活动度可能主要依赖粘连及韧带断裂，而非关节囊撕裂，临床无需刻意追求囊撕裂。但样本量小、缺乏对照组，且未分析撕裂程度与活动度改善的量化关系，未来需更大样本验证。

Conclusion: Manipulation under anesthesia for primary frozen shoulder consistently disrupts intra-articular adhesions and the superior glenohumeral ligament without macroscopic capsular tears, yet achieves significant improvements in range of motion and functional scores at 6 months.

Key points: ①Second-look arthroscopy revealed disruption of intra-articular adhesions and the superior glenohumeral ligament in 100% of patients, with no capsular tears detected. ②SLAP lesions and anteroinferior labral detachments occurred in 12.8% each, none requiring repair. ③Significant improvements in range of motion, VAS, and ASES scores were observed at 6 months postoperatively.

Clinical implications: Restoration of motion after manipulation under anesthesia may rely primarily on disruption of adhesions and the superior glenohumeral ligament rather than capsular tears, suggesting that capsular tearing need not be a surgical goal. However, the small sample size, lack of a control group, and absence of quantitative analysis between tear severity and motion improvement warrant larger studies.
