UCB-MSCs-HA复合植入5年疗效优于微骨折且修复更耐久

More Durable Cartilage Repair and Better Clinical Outcomes at 5 Years Using Allogeneic Umbilical Cord Blood-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells and Hyaluronate Composite Implantation Versus Microfracture for Knee Osteoarthritis.

Arthroscopy · 2026-10-31 · 回顾性亚组分析 · PMID 42965407

一句话结论：在K-L I-III级膝骨关节炎中，同种异体UCB-MSCs-HA复合植入5年时VAS（23.4 vs 46.3）、WOMAC（21.8 vs 37.7）及IKDC评分均显著优于微骨折，且MRI修复质量更耐久。

要点：64例完成5年随访，UCB-MSCs-HA组疼痛与功能评分显著更优；MRI MOCART评分更高且间隔变化小，耐久修复比例更高；微骨折组修复质量随时间下降更明显。

对临床的启示：该研究为UCB-MSCs-HA复合植入治疗膝骨关节炎提供了5年耐久性证据，支持其作为微骨折的替代选择。但为III期试验的回顾性亚组分析，样本量有限（48例完成5年随访），且缺乏组织学验证；临床推广需考虑成本与适应证选择，未来需RCT确认。

Conclusion: At 5 years, allogeneic UCB-MSCs-HA composite implantation provided superior pain and functional outcomes and more durable MRI-based cartilage repair compared with microfracture in K-L grade I-III knee osteoarthritis.

Key points: In 64 patients completing 5-year follow-up, UCB-MSCs-HA group showed significantly better VAS, WOMAC, and IKDC scores; higher MOCART scores with less interval change indicated more durable repair; microfracture group showed greater deterioration over time.

Clinical implications: UCB-MSCs-HA composite implantation offers durable 5-year benefits over microfracture for knee osteoarthritis, supporting its use as an alternative. However, this retrospective subset analysis of a phase 3 trial has limited sample size (48 completed 5-year follow-up) and lacks histological validation; cost and patient selection should be considered, and future RCTs are needed.

This systematic review of 9 studies (2262 ultramarathon runners) found that 3 of 4 studies examining pre-race sleep reported significant associations with race performance; reduced sleep was generally associated with poorer physical and cognitive performance. Greater intra-race sleep duration was consistently associated with longer finish times, reflecting additional nonmoving time. Optimizing pre-race sleep may benefit ultramarathon performance.
