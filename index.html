PRP标准化临床路径治疗K-L I-II级膝骨关节炎：12个月VAS多降2.98分

Clinical Pathway Management for Platelet-Rich Plasma Therapy in Kellgren-Lawrence Grade I and II Knee Osteoarthritis: A Prospective, Multicenter, Double-Blind Randomized Controlled Trial

Am J Sports Med · 2026-10-26 · 随机对照试验 · PMID 42929654

一句话结论：在240例K-L I-II级膝骨关节炎患者中，标准化PRP临床路径较医生主导的常规PRP照护在12个月时VAS疼痛多降低2.98分（95%CI -3.64至-2.32），WOMAC总分多降低8.63分，IKDC多提高10.25分，均P<0.001。

要点：PRP-CP组整合标准化患者选择、质控贫白细胞PRP、超声引导注射、分期康复和系统随访；12个月时三项主要终点均显著优于PRP-C组，组间差异超过MCID阈值，且安全性、满意度和成本数据支持路径化管理。

对临床的启示：该研究提示PRP治疗膝骨关节炎的疗效差异可能部分源于治疗流程的标准化程度，而非单纯PRP制品本身。临床开展PRP时应重视患者选择、制品质控、注射精准性和康复随访的系统整合。局限性包括仅纳入K-L I-II级、12个月随访、开放标签的康复依从性难以完全盲化，以及成本分析受医疗体系影响。

Conclusion: In patients with Kellgren-Lawrence grade I-II knee osteoarthritis, a standardized PRP clinical pathway produced significantly greater 12-month improvements in pain, function, and symptoms than physician-directed conventional PRP care, with mean differences exceeding minimal clinically important thresholds.

Key points: The PRP-CP bundle combined standardized selection, quality-controlled leukocyte-poor PRP, ultrasound-guided injection, staged rehabilitation, and systematic follow-up. At 12 months, all three primary endpoints favored PRP-CP over PRP-C, with between-group differences exceeding MCID thresholds; safety, satisfaction, and cost data supported pathway management.

Clinical implications: These findings suggest that variability in PRP outcomes for knee OA may partly reflect the degree of standardization in the treatment process rather than the PRP product alone. Clinicians should emphasize systematic integration of patient selection, product quality control, injection accuracy, and rehabilitation follow-up. Limitations include restriction to K-L I-II, 12-month follow-up, difficulty fully blinding rehabilitation adherence, and cost analyses that vary across health systems.
