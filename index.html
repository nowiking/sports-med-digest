女性足球ACL损伤：职业球员发生率是14-15岁组的15.6倍

Incidence of Anterior Cruciate Ligament Injuries in Female Soccer Players Across Age Groups and Competition Levels

Orthop J Sports Med · 2026-09 · 描述性流行病学研究 · PMID 43036538

一句话结论：西班牙马拉加足球 federation 2020-2024年4937名≥14岁女性球员共发生64例ACL损伤，总发生率随年龄与竞技水平递增：职业球员0.3125例/1000小时，16-18岁组0.1020例/1000小时，14-15岁组0.0201例/1000小时；相对风险分别为15.62与5.08，79.7%发生于比赛、82.8%为非接触性。

要点：①职业球员ACL发生率0.3125例/1000小时（95%CI 0.0644-0.9133），为14-15岁组的15.62倍（95%CI 4.36-55.7）；②16-18岁组发生率0.1020例/1000小时，相对风险5.08（95%CI 2.44-11.5）；③64例中79.7%发生于比赛、82.8%为非接触性损伤。

对临床的启示：该研究提示女性足球ACL预防应重点覆盖16岁以上、尤其职业/高水平竞技人群，并将比赛场景与非接触机制作为神经肌肉控制训练的核心靶点。近期女性足球ACL议题持续升温，本研究提供了南欧 federation 体系的年龄-水平分层基线，可与北欧及北美数据对照。局限在于单地区、回顾性识别、可能低估轻伤与暴露时间估算误差，且未校正训练负荷、月经周期等混杂因素。

Conclusion: ACL injury incidence in female soccer rises steeply with age and competition level, with professional players showing a 15.6-fold higher rate than 14- to 15-year-olds and most injuries occurring in matches and without contact.

Key points: ①Professional players: 0.3125 injuries per 1000 hours (95%CI 0.0644-0.9133), 15.62-fold vs the 14- to 15-year group (95%CI 4.36-55.7); ②Ages 16-18 years: 0.1020 per 1000 hours, RR 5.08 (95%CI 2.44-11.5); ③79.7% occurred in matches and 82.8% were noncontact.

Clinical implications: Prevention resources for female soccer should target players aged 16 years and older, especially professional/high-level athletes, with match-scenario and noncontact-mechanism neuromuscular training as the core. This single-region retrospective federation study provides age- and level-stratified baseline data but may underestimate mild injuries and exposure time, and does not adjust for training load or menstrual cycle.
