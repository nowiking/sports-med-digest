给 ACL 重建加 PRP/干细胞「增强」：临床和影像结局都没改善

Orthobiologic Augmentation for Anterior Cruciate Ligament Reconstruction Is Not Associated With Improved Clinical or Radiographic Outcomes

Arthroscopy · 2026-10-05 · 系统综述+Meta · PMID 42832231

一句话结论：9 项 RCT、543 例患者的系统综述与 Meta 分析，评估正生物学增强（PRP、骨髓穿刺浓缩物、间充质干细胞）对 ACLR 结局的影响。结果显示：正生物学增强与 ACLR 临床结局或移植物成熟度（影像）的改善均无显著相关。

要点：检索 PubMed、EMBASE、Web of Science，纳入比较 ACLR ± 正生物学的 RCT，排除仅用于供区者。对同一种正生物学生物学且 ≥3 项研究的结局用随机效应模型做 Meta 分析（SMD）。

对临床的启示：PRP、BMAC、干细胞给 ACL 重建「加点料」在临床上很流行，但这篇 I 级证据的 Meta 给了一个清醒的结论：无论临床评分还是移植物成熟度，增强组与对照组都没差别——与「局部微环境本已足够、外源因子边际效用有限」的生物学逻辑一致。临床意义：不必为 ACLR 常规加用正生物学增强，患者也不该为此额外付费。局限是仅 9 项 RCT、存在异质性，且不同生物制剂与剂量方案混杂。

Conclusion: A systematic review and meta-analysis of 9 RCTs (543 patients) evaluated orthobiologic augmentation (PRP, bone marrow aspirate concentrate, mesenchymal stem cells) for ACLR. Orthobiologics were not significantly associated with improved clinical outcomes or radiographic graft maturation.

Key points: PubMed/EMBASE/Web of Science search for RCTs comparing ACLR with vs without orthobiologics (applied to the graft, not the donor site). Meta-analysis (random-effects, SMD) for outcomes with ≥3 studies using the same biologic.

Clinical implications: PRP/BMAC/stem-cell 'boosters' for ACLR are popular, but this Level-I meta-analysis delivers a sobering verdict: no difference in clinical scores or graft maturation between augmented and control groups—consistent with the biology that the local milieu is already sufficient. Practical takeaway: do not routinely add orthobiologics to ACLR, and patients should not pay extra for it. Limitations: only 9 RCTs, heterogeneity, and mixed biologic/dose regimens.
