ACL重建后6个月离心腘绳肌/向心股四头肌比值每增10单位：2年内翻修风险显著降低

第 33 期 · 2026-11-08

本期主打一项基于3458例ACL重建患者的大样本队列研究：术后6个月离心腘绳肌与向心股四头肌力量比值每提高10单位，2年内翻修风险显著下降，提示力量平衡评估应作为重返运动决策的量化指标。同期多项研究聚焦半月板与关节镜技术：外侧半月板过度活动预测评分、半月板中央化专家共识、双平面截骨降低铰链骨折等，为运动损伤的精准诊疗提供新证据。

ACL重建后6个月离心腘绳肌/向心股四头肌比值每增10单位：2年内翻修风险显著降低

Greater eccentric hamstrings to concentric quadriceps strength ratio at 6 months after anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction is associated with reduced odds of revision within 2 years

Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc · 2026-11-06 · 回顾性队列 · PMID 42994101

一句话结论：在3458例初次ACL重建患者中，术后6个月离心腘绳肌/向心股四头肌力量比值每增加10单位，2年内翻修风险显著降低（校正后OR及AUC见原文），且该比值与单腿跳距离对称指数相关。

要点：①纳入2005-2021年3458例腘绳肌腱ACL重建患者，49.5%女性，平均年龄28.8岁；②6个月等速测试计算混合HQ比值，翻修组比值显著更低；③校正年龄和伤前Tegner后，比值每增10单位翻修风险显著下降；④比值与单腿跳对称指数呈正相关。

对临床的启示：该研究为ACL重建后重返运动决策提供了可量化的力量平衡阈值：6个月时离心腘绳肌/向心股四头肌比值不仅是功能对称性的替代指标，更与2年内翻修风险独立相关。临床应在康复中期常规行等速测试，并将HQ比值纳入重返运动标准。局限为回顾性设计、单中心注册数据，且未明确比值的最佳截断值，需前瞻性验证。

Conclusion: In 3458 patients after primary ACL reconstruction, each 10-unit increase in the mixed eccentric hamstrings-to-concentric quadriceps strength ratio at 6 months was associated with significantly reduced odds of revision within 2 years, and the ratio correlated with single-leg hop limb symmetry.

Key points: ①3458 patients (49.5% female, mean age 28.8 years) undergoing hamstring autograft ACL reconstruction (2005-2021); ②the mixed HQ-strength ratio at 6 months was lower in patients who later underwent revision; ③after adjustment, each 10-unit increase in the ratio was associated with reduced odds of revision; ④the ratio correlated with single-leg hop LSI.

Clinical implications: This study provides a quantifiable strength-balance target for return-to-sport decision-making after ACL reconstruction: the eccentric hamstrings-to-concentric quadriceps ratio at 6 months is independently associated with 2-year revision risk, beyond functional symmetry. Isokinetic testing should be routinely incorporated into mid-rehabilitation assessment and return-to-sport criteria. Limitations include the retrospective single-registry design and the lack of a defined optimal cut-off, warranting prospective validation.
