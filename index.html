胫骨平台几何形态与女性运动员对侧ACL再伤无关联

Prospective Polynomial Characterization of Tibial Plateau Geometry Shows No Association With Contralateral ACL Injury in Female Athletes

Sports Health · 2026-11-11 · 前瞻性队列（II级） · PMID 43012382

一句话结论：在199例首次ACL损伤并重建的女性运动员中，对侧膝胫骨平台软骨面几何形态（五阶多项式参数）与4年内对侧ACL再伤无显著关联（再伤12例 vs 对照24例）。

要点：①纳入199例首次ACL重建后重返运动的女性运动员，对侧膝行双膝MRI；②以五阶多项式拟合胫骨平台最大凹陷处软骨面几何；③4年内对侧ACL再伤12例，匹配未再伤24例；④混合效应回归显示两组多项式函数无显著差异。

对临床的启示：该结果提示，首次ACL损伤的形态学危险因素（如胫骨平台后倾、软骨面凹陷）不能直接外推至对侧再伤风险，临床不应仅凭对侧膝形态决定是否限制重返运动。对侧再伤更可能由可干预因素（神经肌肉控制、暴露量、康复质量）驱动，应强化再伤预防训练与重返运动标准。局限：再伤事件仅12例，统计效能有限；仅女性、单一中心，多项式方法对微小形态差异敏感性未知。

Conclusion: Tibial plateau cartilage surface geometry was not associated with contralateral ACL injury in female athletes after primary ACL reconstruction and return to sport.

Key points: ①199 female athletes with first-time ACL reconstruction underwent bilateral MRI; ②fifth-order polynomials characterized tibial plateau cartilage geometry; ③12 sustained contralateral ACL injury within 4 years, matched to 24 uninjured; ④no significant group differences in polynomial functions.

Clinical implications: Morphologic risk factors for first-time ACL injury should not be extrapolated to contralateral re-injury risk; decisions on return to sport should not rely solely on contralateral knee geometry. Re-injury prevention should target modifiable factors such as neuromuscular control, exposure, and rehabilitation quality. Limitations include only 12 re-injury events, female-only single-center design, and uncertain sensitivity of polynomial modeling.
