肩袖修复加生物补片：再撕裂率降到三分之一，肌腱更厚

Biologic Graft Augmentation Is Associated With Lower Retear Rates and Greater Tendon Thickness Compared With Rotator Cuff Repair Without Augmentation

Arthroscopy · 2026-10-08 · 系统综述+Meta · PMID 42850980

一句话结论：9 项 RCT、556 例患者的 Meta 分析（I–II 级证据），比较肩袖修复 ± 生物移植物增强。结果显示：生物移植物增强显著降低再撕裂率（RR 0.31，约降七成），且术后肌腱厚度更大；ASES、Constant、UCLA 评分也见改善。

要点：按 PRISMA 检索 PubMed、Scopus、Embase、Cochrane，纳入比较生物移植物增强 + 标准肩袖修复的 RCT（随访 >1 年），随机效应模型计算均差与 95%CI。

对临床的启示：这一期和上一期主打形成了教科书式的对照：上一期「ACL 重建打 PRP/干细胞没用」，这一期「肩袖修复加生物补片再撕裂率降到三分之一」。差异不在「增强」这个概念，而在载体——肩袖用的是结构性的生物补片（提供力学支架、引导组织长入），ACL 用的是游离的细胞因子（PRP/干细胞）。这提示：单纯的生物学信号可能不够，需要一个能承受载荷、引导再生的结构。临床意义：肩袖修复的补片增强有 I 级证据支持，值得在高再撕裂风险（巨大撕裂、退变重）患者中考虑。局限是仅 9 项 RCT、补片类型与厂家混杂。

Conclusion: A meta-analysis of 9 RCTs (556 patients, Level I–II) compared rotator-cuff repair with vs without biologic graft augmentation. Augmentation significantly reduced retear rates (RR 0.31, ~70% lower) with greater tendon thickness, plus improved ASES, Constant and UCLA scores.

Key points: PRISMA search of PubMed/Scopus/Embase/Cochrane for RCTs comparing biologic graft augmentation plus standard cuff repair (follow-up >1 y); random-effects models for mean differences.

Clinical implications: This issue and the last form a textbook contrast: last time 'PRP/stem cells fail in ACL,' this time 'biologic graft augmentation cuts cuff retears to one-third.' The difference is not the concept of augmentation but the carrier—cuff augmentation uses a structural patch (mechanical scaffold guiding ingrowth), while ACL used free cytokines (PRP/stem cells). This suggests biologic signaling alone may be insufficient without a load-bearing, regenerative structure. Clinical meaning: cuff augmentation has Level-I support and is worth considering in high-retear-risk patients (massive/degenerate tears). Limitations: only 9 RCTs, mixed patch types.

原文 / Source：doi.org/10.1002/arj.70619
