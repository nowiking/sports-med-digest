ACL 移植物泡万古霉素：全身血清浓度只有中毒阈值的千分之一

Peak Serum Vancomycin Levels Are Achieved Between 6 and 24 Hours After Implantation of Vancomycin-Soaked Anterior Cruciate Ligament Grafts

Arthroscopy · 2026-09-28 · 血清药代动力学研究 · PMID 42803685

一句话结论：21 例（17 男 4 女，中位 36 岁）单纯 ACL 重建或二期翻修，腘绳肌/股四头肌腱移植物万古霉素浸泡。术后 48 小时 6 次抽血监测：血清万古霉素峰值出现在 6–8 小时与 24 小时，中位均为 0.0050 μg/mL，48 小时仍可测到 0.0021 μg/mL。所有样本最高浓度仅 0.704 μg/mL，远低于中毒阈值 15–20 μg/mL。浸泡时间与最高血清浓度显著相关（r=0.497，P=.022）。

要点：2023-10 至 2024-01，术前、松止血带后 10 分钟、1h、6–8h、24h、48h 六次采血，串联质谱 + 高效液相色谱分析万古霉素浓度，Spearman 相关分析浸泡时间与浓度的关系。

对临床的启示：局部万古霉素浸泡移植物是公认的防感染策略，但「会不会全身吸收中毒」的顾虑一直存在。这篇用实测数据打消了它——即便浸泡时间越长吸收入血越多（r=0.497），最高浓度 0.704 μg/mL 也只有中毒阈值的 1/20 到 1/30。临床意义：可以放心用，但浸泡时间不宜无限延长（浓度与时间正相关），且这只针对常规剂量、肾功能正常者；肾功能不全者全身清除下降，仍需谨慎。

Conclusion: 21 patients (17 men, 4 women; median age 36) undergoing isolated ACLR or 2-stage revision with vancomycin-soaked hamstring/quadriceps grafts. Six samples over 48 h: peak serum vancomycin occurred at 6–8 h and 24 h (median 0.0050 μg/mL each), still detectable at 48 h (0.0021 μg/mL). The maximum concentration across all samples was only 0.704 μg/mL, far below the toxic threshold of 15–20 μg/mL. Soaking time correlated with maximum serum concentration (r=0.497, P=.022).

Key points: Oct 2023–Jan 2024; six samples (preop, 10 min after tourniquet release, 1 h, 6–8 h, 24 h, 48 h); vancomycin measured by tandem mass spectrometry and HPLC; Spearman correlation with soaking time.

Clinical implications: Local vancomycin soaking is an accepted infection-prevention strategy, yet systemic-absorption concerns linger. This study resolves them: even though longer soaking increases systemic levels (r=0.497), the peak of 0.704 μg/mL is only 1/20–1/30 of the toxic threshold. Practical takeaway: use it confidently, but avoid indefinite soaking (concentration correlates with time), and note this applies to standard dosing with normal renal function—renal impairment warrants caution.
