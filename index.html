术前肩胛肌预康复改善肩袖修复术后功能与肩胛控制：RCT

Does scapular muscle training program preoperatively improve the functional outcomes and decrease coracoid pain?: A randomized controlled trial.

J ISAKOS · 2026-11-03 · Randomized Controlled Trial · PMID 42976660

一句话结论：在50例拟行关节镜下肩袖修复的患者中，4-6周术前肩胛肌预康复较对照组显著改善术后6个月肩胛肌力对称性、肩胛控制及患者报告结局，并减轻喙突疼痛。

要点：预康复组术后6个月ASES、Constant、SPADI等评分均优于对照组，肩胛肌等长肌力对称性改善，喙突压痛减少。两组均较术前改善，但预康复组改善幅度更大。

对临床的启示：该研究支持将结构化肩胛肌预康复纳入肩袖修复术前管理，尤其对肩胛运动障碍患者。但样本量较小（50例），随访仅6个月，且未报告再撕裂率，长期获益需进一步验证。临床可考虑对肩胛控制不良者进行4-6周预康复。

Conclusion: A 4-6 week preoperative scapular-focused prehabilitation program improved periscapular muscle strength symmetry, scapular control, and patient-reported outcomes at 6 months after arthroscopic rotator cuff repair compared with control.

Key points: Prehabilitation group showed better ASES, Constant, and SPADI scores at 6 months, improved periscapular strength symmetry, and reduced coracoid tenderness. Both groups improved from baseline, but prehabilitation had greater gains.

Clinical implications: Structured scapular prehabilitation should be considered before rotator cuff repair, especially for patients with scapular dyskinesis. However, the small sample size (50) and short follow-up (6 months) limit generalizability; long-term re-tear rates are needed.
