休闲滑雪ACL断裂集中于假期初段与午前：138例分析

Risk Period for Anterior Cruciate Ligament Rupture During Skiing: A Multicenter Epidemiological Study

Sports Health · 2026-11-18 · 多中心回顾性观察研究 · PMID 43048910

一句话结论：138例MRI确诊的休闲滑雪ACL断裂中，46%发生于滑雪假期前段、64.6%发生于午前，83.3%为中级或高级滑雪者，58.3%在雪况一般或较差时受伤，提示神经肌肉适应不足与疲劳是重要可干预因素。

要点：①46%损伤发生于滑雪假期开始阶段，64.6%在午前，支持“适应不足与疲劳”假说；②83.3%为中级或高级滑雪者，提示经验不能完全防护；③58.3%在雪况一般或较差时受伤，能见度无明显关联；④28.5%合并损伤，以半月板后角纵裂为主。

对临床的启示：本研究为雪季ACL预防提供了明确的时间窗口：假期首日与午前应强化热身、控制滑行强度并安排间歇休息，雪况不佳时主动降速。对运动医学医生而言，可在雪季前筛查中针对性开展神经肌肉控制训练与疲劳管理教育。局限在于回顾性设计、伤因自报偏倚、缺乏暴露人时数据，无法计算真实发病率，结论需前瞻性队列验证。

Conclusion: In recreational skiers, ACL ruptures clustered at the beginning of ski vacations (46%) and before midday (64.6%), with most injuries in intermediate or expert skiers and under average or poor snow conditions, suggesting insufficient neuromuscular adaptation and fatigue as modifiable risk factors.

Key points: ①46% of ACL ruptures occurred early in ski vacations and 64.6% before midday; ②83.3% occurred in intermediate or expert skiers; ③58.3% occurred under average or poor snow conditions, with no clear association with visibility; ④28.5% had concomitant injuries, mainly vertical medial meniscus tears.

Clinical implications: These findings define a practical prevention window: intensify warm-up, modulate skiing intensity, and schedule rest breaks on the first vacation day and before midday, and reduce speed when snow conditions are poor. Clinicians can target neuromuscular control training and fatigue management in preseason screening. Limitations include the retrospective design, self-reported injury mechanisms, and lack of exposure data precluding true incidence estimates; prospective cohort validation is needed.
